# Feel the Number · 量感

Guess how big a number really is — then see it, life-size, in your own room. PICO OS 6 (Swan), Mixed Stage.

A question says "1 kg of CO₂". Pull a ball out of your floor to the size you think it is, tap **Reveal**, and the
true volume grows 1:1 from the same spot, with your error in volume and up to three stars. Things bigger than your
room are cut by its walls and ceiling; **Wrap me** puts you inside one.

- 20 questions in four sets (CO₂, one kilo, everyday, too big), from a 3.7 cm cube of gold to a 21 m ball of CO₂
- your real room from plane detection, or a virtual room you can size and turn
- gaze + pinch or controller ray; stick to resize and turn
- Chinese and English, sound on/off, fully offline

Inspired by Real World Visuals' life-size CO₂ bubbles. Fonts: Noto Sans / Noto Sans SC (SIL OFL 1.1, `third_party/`).
